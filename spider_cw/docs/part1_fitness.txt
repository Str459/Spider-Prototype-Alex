PART 1 - FITNESS FUNCTION
=========================
Files: part1_ga/gait_fitness.m (gait), part1_ga/fitness_config.m (settings
and weights), part1_ga/support_margin.m (stability), part1_ga/pose_fitness.m
(Stage A single pose).

1. What "a good gait" means here
--------------------------------
The provided model has geometry but no physics, and the spec says we do not
need to model physics. So the fitness judges a gait geometrically, in the
body frame (+x forward, +z up), on all 300 frames of one cycle:

  GOAL         walk forward as far as possible per cycle
  REQUIREMENTS planted feet on the ground, nothing below the ground,
               swinging feet lifted clear, at least 4 feet down at all
               times, body statically stable, feet not colliding,
               no sideways drift, little slipping

2. How each term is measured
----------------------------
Ground height g = groundZ = -0.4 (the body hangs 0.4 above the ground).

progress      Planted feet are assumed not to slide on the ground. So if the
              planted feet move backwards relative to the body by d, the body
              has moved forwards by d. Per frame, body velocity = minus the
              mean displacement of the feet planted in this frame AND the
              next. Summed over the cycle = distance walked per cycle.
lateral       |sum of sideways velocity| over the cycle (drift).
slip          How much the planted feet disagree with that average motion.
              Zero would mean a perfectly rigid set of footholds.
groundErr     Mean |z - g| of planted feet.
penetration   Mean depth of any foot below the ground.
clearanceDef  For each leg, how far the top of its swing falls short of
              `clearance` (0.15). Stops "shuffling" steps.
minMargin     Static stability: the smallest distance, over the cycle, from
              the body centre (assumed centre of mass) to the edge of the
              polygon made by the planted feet. Negative = would tip over.
              support_margin.m sorts planted feet by angle round the body and
              takes the signed distance to each edge, vectorised over the
              whole population.
instability   Mean shortfall below the required margin `minMargin` (0.3).
stanceDef     FRACTION of frames with fewer than `minStance` (4) feet down.
collision     Mean shortfall of the distance between neighbouring feet below
              `footGap` (0.35), summed over the 8 neighbour pairs.
effort        Mean squared joint speed: a small energy-like cost that prefers
              smooth, unhurried motion when nothing else matters.

3. How the terms are combined ("goal first")
--------------------------------------------
  score = 3 * progress  +  1 * min(minMargin, 0.3)
        - 20 groundErr - 30 penetration - 10 clearanceDef - 20 instability
        - 50 stanceDef - 1 slip - 2 lateral - 20 collision - 0.01 effort

* Progress is the only term that keeps paying.
* Every requirement costs nothing once it is met. A plain weighted sum of
  "progress + stability" was tried in an earlier prototype: the GA bought a
  slightly wider stance at the cost of all forward motion and stood still.
  Capping the stability reward at 0.3 turns stability into a requirement
  rather than a prize.
* Weights are sized so that the typical size of each violation is worth
  roughly as much as a useful amount of progress, e.g. a planted foot 5 cm
  off the ground (20 x 0.05 = 1) costs about a third of a unit of walking.
* stanceDef counts frames, with a heavy weight, because an earlier version
  averaged the shortfall and one 3-foot frame in 300 cost just 0.008: the GA
  happily accepted short dips to 3 feet.

4. Tuning notes (what was tried and why)
----------------------------------------
* Frames scored: scoring only 60 frames per cycle was 5x faster but brief
  3-foot moments fell between samples and showed up in the 300-frame
  output. Scoring the exact 300 frames that are exported costs about 13 s
  per run, which is affordable.
* Slip weight 1 vs 3 (5 seeds each): weight 3 reduced slip only from about
  1.25 to 0.95 per cycle, while distance fell from about 3.9 to 3.7. Slip is
  mostly built into the encoding: a coxa turn moves a foot along an arc, and
  legs that point diagonally move their feet partly sideways. The GA's
  response is visible in the evolved gaits: it turns the front and rear legs
  towards the sides, where an arc is closest to a straight backwards line.
  We kept weight 1 and report the slip honestly.

5. Target-speed mode (used to make Part 2 training data)
--------------------------------------------------------
fitness_config('targetSpeed') = s replaces 3 * progress with
-5 * |progress - s|. Running the GA for several s produces a family of gaits
at different speeds. The NN in Part 2 learns to map (phase in the cycle,
desired speed) to 24 joint angles.

6. Stage A: single standing pose (pose_fitness.m)
-------------------------------------------------
  score = 2 * min(margin, 1.5) - 20 groundErr - 30 penetration
        - 2 symmetry - 20 collision
with all 8 feet expected on the ground and symmetry measured as the mean
mismatch between each left leg and its mirrored right partner.

7. Assumptions and limitations
------------------------------
* Centre of mass at the body centre; legs are massless.
* "Stable" means statically stable (no tipping if frozen). No dynamics,
  friction or torque limits are modelled.
* Foot collisions only check feet, not whole legs.
* Progress assumes no slipping; the slip term measures how far that is from
  true.
