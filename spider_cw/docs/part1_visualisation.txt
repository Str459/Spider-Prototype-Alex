PART 1 - VISUALISATION TOOLS
============================
Every run of run_gait_ga writes these to outputs/part1/<name>/. They answer
two questions: "is the GA working?" and "is the result a sensible gait?".

IS THE GA WORKING?
------------------
Console log (ga_run.m)
    Every 10 generations: best and mean fitness, diversity, sigma. Shows
    progress live while a run is going.

fitness.png (plot_ga_history.m)
    Top: best, mean and median fitness per generation.
      - Best never falls: elitism keeps the best chromosome.
      - Mean following best means selection is pulling the population
        towards good solutions.
      - Long flat stretches followed by jumps = the search was stuck and a
        mutation or crossover found a way out.
    Bottom: diversity (mean standard deviation of each gene / its range)
    and the adaptive mutation step sigma.
      - Diversity falling quickly to ~0 would mean premature convergence.
      - Sigma shrinks while the GA keeps improving and grows after 15
        stalled generations, to explore again. When it grows, the mean
        fitness drops (the population spreads out) while the best stays.

components.png (plot_ga_history.m)
    Each fitness term of the best chromosome per generation. Shows the order
    in which the GA solved the problem: typically feet onto the ground and
    collisions removed first, then enough feet down, then stride grows.

outputs/part1/experiments/ (run_experiments.m)
    convergence.png: average best-fitness curves of each variant, one panel
    per GA component. final_scores.png: final fitness of every single run.
    summary.csv / runs.csv: the numbers.

IS THE RESULT A SENSIBLE GAIT?
------------------------------
pose_snapshots.png (animate_gait.m)
    Six 3D renderings through one cycle, using the lecturer's colours
    (coxa black, femur blue, tibia red). Green feet are planted, open red
    feet are swinging.

gait.gif (animate_gait.m)
    Two cycles of walking. The body is moved forward by the distance the
    planted feet push it, and planted footprints are left on the ground as
    grey dots. If a planted foot truly holds still, its footprints pile up
    in one place; a smeared trail shows slip.

foot_heights.png (plot_gait_analysis.m)
    Top: height of each foot above the ground. Planted feet sit exactly on
    0, swinging feet rise above the dotted clearance line.
    Bottom: gait diagram, black = planted. The diagonal bands show the
    wave of steps running down each side and the two sides alternating.

joint_angles.png (plot_gait_analysis.m)
    All 24 joint angles over the cycle. Coxa angles ramp linearly during
    stance and return smoothly in swing; femur and tibia are flat in stance
    and bump during swing.

stability.png (plot_gait_analysis.m)
    Stability margin over the cycle (must stay above the red line), number
    of feet down (never below 4) and the body's forward speed.

lecturer_view.png (run_gait_ga.m)
    Frame 1 of best_gait.csv drawn by the PROVIDED plot_spider_pose.m,
    unchanged. Shows our exported angles plug straight into the lecturer's
    model. test_part1.m also checks our fast kinematics against the
    provided forward_leg_kinematics2.m (max error about 1e-14).

Stage A (run_pose_ga) writes fitness.png, components.png, pose.png and
lecturer_view.png for the standing pose.
