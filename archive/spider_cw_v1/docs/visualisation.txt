VISUALISATION TOOLS (part1_ga/plots.py)
=======================================
All figures are produced by part1_ga/plots.py (self-test: `python -m part1_ga.plots` from spider_cw/).

fitness.png  - plot_fitness
  Best, mean and worst fitness per generation. A working GA shows the best curve rising quickly
  and then levelling off (convergence). The mean following the best upwards shows selection is
  pushing the whole population; a gap that never closes means diversity is being kept, a gap
  that collapses to zero early warns of premature convergence.

components.png  - plot_components
  Each term of the fitness function for the best individual, one panel per term. Shows which
  part of the objective is being improved (e.g. distance travelled rising, foot slip falling)
  and exposes trade-offs, or a single term dominating the score.

sigma.png  - plot_mutation_sigma
  Mutation step size per generation. Evidence that the adaptive mutation is doing something:
  sigma should shrink as the search fine-tunes, and rise again if progress stalls.

snapshots.png  - plot_pose_snapshots
  3D frame-0 pose of the best individual at generation 0, ~1/3, ~2/3 and the end, drawn with the
  lecturer's MATLAB colour scheme (coxa black, femur blue, tibia red, head = red triangle).
  The pose should go from a random tangle to a symmetric, plausible standing posture.

joints.png  - plot_joint_angles
  The 3 joint angles of each leg over the 300 frames. Confirms the gait is smooth and loops
  (frame 300 meets frame 0) and lets you compare timing (phase) between legs.

feet.png  - plot_foot_heights
  Top: height of each foot over time. Bottom: footfall (gait) diagram - a black bar while a
  foot is in stance (within 0.1 of the lowest foot that frame). A good walking gait has legs
  lifting and landing in alternating groups, with several feet down at every instant for
  support; a leg that never touches down or a gap with almost no feet down is a bad sign.

gait.gif  - animate_gait
  Animation of the evolved gait in 3D (every few frames), the most direct check that the
  spider actually walks rather than just scoring well on paper.

compare.png  - compare_runs
  For the operator experiments: mean best-fitness curve over several seeds per configuration,
  with a shaded band of +/- 1 standard deviation. Runs that stopped early keep their final
  value. A configuration is better if its curve is higher; a narrow band means it is reliably
  better, while overlapping bands mean the difference may be down to random seed.
