FITNESS FUNCTION (part1_ga/fitness.py)
======================================

Purpose. The GA needs one number saying how good a 300-frame gait is. The brief asks for poses
that are plausible, stable and goal-directed, but not for full physics, so the score is computed
from the kinematics only: decode the genes into 300 poses (see docs/encoding.txt), compute every
joint position, and judge the foot trajectories against a simple ground/stance model.
Score = sum of weighted terms, higher is better. Each term is a separate function so its effect
can be plotted and argued for.

Ground and stance model
-----------------------
The body is fixed at the origin (z = 0) in its own frame. In every frame the ground is the plane
through the lowest foot, so body height = -(lowest foot z). A foot within STANCE_TOL = 0.05 of
that plane is in STANCE, otherwise it is SWINGING. A frame is STATICALLY STABLE when >= 4 feet
are down and the body centre lies inside their convex support polygon (signed margin > 0; the
margin is computed with the support function min_u max_i u.foot_i over 32 directions u, which
gives the distance to the nearest edge when inside and minus the distance when outside, without
building hulls, so it vectorises). Stance feet are assumed not to slip, so when they move
backwards relative to the body, the body moves forwards by the same amount:
body velocity = -(mean displacement of feet that stay grounded over the step). A step only
counts if >= 4 feet stay down through it AND the body is statically stable at both ends of it -
an unstable body would tip over, not be carried forwards. The cycle is periodic (frame 300 ->
frame 0) so the step from the last frame back to the first is included.

Terms (components dict = unweighted values)
-------------------------------------------
progress      Forward (+X) distance per cycle. This is the goal. With target_speed (distance per
              cycle, for Part 2's multi-speed training data) it becomes -|distance - target|, so
              the GA hits a chosen speed instead of maximising. distance(pop) returns the raw value.
stability     A constraint, not a prize. Reward: average of mean and worst-frame margin (a gait
              is only as safe as its worst moment), capped at MARGIN_CAP = 0.2. Penalty:
              UNSTABLE_PENALTY (5) x fraction of frames that are not statically stable.
clearance     The body must ride at least 0.5 above the ground (no belly dragging) at a steady
              height (std of height penalised: no bobbing), no knee may go below the ground plane,
              and neighbouring feet must stay 0.3 apart (leg collision).
slip          Grounded feet on a rigid body all move with the same velocity; if they do not, they
              are skating. Penalises the RMS spread of stance-foot steps summed over the cycle,
              plus sideways (Y) distance travelled. Both are distances, comparable to progress.
coordination  Legs L1..L4,R4..R1 form a ring, so legs i and i+1 (mod 8) are exactly the neighbour
              pairs (front/back on each side, plus L1-R1 and L4-R4). Penalises the fraction of
              frames where two neighbours are in the air together. Standing still scores 0 (best)
              on purpose: rewarding motion is progress's job, so this cannot be farmed by wiggling.
smoothness    Mean joint acceleration (scaled so a sine of amplitude A scores ~A) plus time spent
              within 0.1 rad of the joint limits. The sine encoding is already smooth, so this
              mainly discourages needlessly large swings and joints jammed against their stops.
lift          Every leg must step: each foot's peak height above the ground over the cycle,
              capped at LIFT_CAP = 0.15, averaged over the 8 legs, counted only for feet that
              also touch down. Max contribution 2 x 0.15 = 0.3. A leg that never touches down
              costs IDLE_PENALTY = 4 x its share of the 8 legs (x weight 2 = -1 per idle leg).

Weights and tuning
------------------
WEIGHTS = progress 3, stability 1, clearance 2, slip 1, coordination 1, smoothness 0.2, lift 2.
Reference gaits (python -m part1_ga.fitness):
  2000 random chromosomes   median about -11 (stability about -7.6: random poses are never stable)
  hand-built tetrapod       3.94 (progress 1.75, slip -1.73, stability 0.2, lift 0.15)
  zero-amplitude standing   0.20 (stability 0.2, every other term 0)

How the weights got here (each change answers a measured failure)
  * progress 1.0 -> 1.5 (first version): with equal weights the hand tetrapod scored below
    standing, because a coxa sweep moves each foot on an arc, so stance feet always skate.
  * The GA still stood still on every seed. Scaling the hand tetrapod's amplitudes by k showed
    a dip: score 1.00 at k=0, 0.71 at 0.1, 0.74 at 0.25, 1.25 at 0.5, 1.81 at 1. Small strides
    drag feet before they lift clear, so standing is a real local optimum. Dragging IS worse than
    standing, so the dip was kept; the encoding was changed instead (docs/encoding.txt) and:
  * MARGIN_CAP 1.0 -> 0.2 and progress 1.5 -> 3: standing with 8 feet spread earned a full
    stability point, as much as a good walk. Stability is now a requirement, progress the goal.
    The k-scan with these weights is monotone (0.20, 0.28 at k=0.25, 1.67 at 0.5, 3.64 at 1).
  * The GA then found fast walkers that leaned on 3 feet for part of the cycle (8 seeds:
    progress 1.4-2.0 but stability -0.3..-0.6). Fix: progress only on statically stable steps
    and a 5x penalty per unstable fraction of the cycle. Afterwards every evolved walker keeps
    >= 4 feet down with the body inside the support polygon at every frame.
  * About a third of runs still ended standing still. No random chromosome is statically
    stable (0 of 5000), so the GA first reaches stability by shrinking every amplitude
    (mean 0.22 -> 0.05 rad within 40 generations); amplitude 0 is a bound, i.e. sticky, and
    walking from there needs several genes to change together. Fix: the lift term, so stepping
    while stable beats standing. Its first version paid feet held permanently in the air (one
    run stood on 6 feet with 2 raised); lift now only counts feet that also touch down.
    With that fitness 4/5 seeds walked 1.39-2.83 per cycle, but seed 2 walked on 6 legs with
    both hind legs held 1.77 above the ground all cycle, and seed 0 stood on 6 with L2/R2 raised:
    an idle leg only cost ~0.25 through coordination. Fix: IDLE_PENALTY (-1 per idle leg).
    Result with the final fitness (default GAConfig, seeds 0-4): 5/5 seeds walk 1.91-3.67 per
    cycle on all 8 legs (each leg in stance 50-59% of the cycle), >= 4 feet down at every frame.
  * smoothness 0.2 (chosen, not tuned): roughly proportional to swing amplitude, which a walk
    needs, so it is a tie-breaker rather than a competitor to progress.
Alternatives tried and rejected (pop 100, 300 generations, 5 seeds unless stated):
  softer stability penalty 1 (instead of 5): 3/5 walk but one walker leans on 2 feet; 2: 1/5.
  minimum amplitudes (coxa 0.1, femur 0.05 rad) instead of lift: 4/6, but the rest settle on
  "shuffles" with the femur phase at 0 or pi (feet lift at both stroke ends, net progress 0).
  penalising the 4th-lowest foot's height (a continuous stability gradient): 1/6.
  population 300: 0/5 reach 1.0 per cycle in 300 generations (slower per generation).
  mutation rate 0.15: 3/5; tournament size 2: 1/5.
Exploits found with a 15 s random-mutation hill climber used as an adversary (in the self-check):
  1. From random starts it "walked" 4.5 per cycle by paddling one or two feet with the others in
     the air. Fix: steps need >= 3 (now 4, stable) grounded feet.
  2. Refining the hand gait, it swung neighbouring legs to within 0.04 of each other (legs
     colliding). Fix: the neighbouring-feet gap penalty in clearance (worst frame counts).
The self-check asserts that the climbed gaits keep >= 3 feet down and feet > 0.1 apart.

Trade-offs and limitations
--------------------------
* Kinematic only: no masses, torques, friction or momentum. "Stable" means statically stable
  (centre of mass over the support polygon), which is conservative for a slow walker.
* The ground is defined by the lowest foot each frame, so the body is implicitly allowed to rise
  and fall; clearance penalises that variation rather than simulating it. Body pitch/roll is
  ignored (feet are judged in the body frame).
* Stance is a hard threshold (0.05), so scores change in small steps when a foot crosses it.
* Collision checking is between neighbouring feet only, not whole leg segments.
* One sine per joint cannot give a perfectly flat, straight stance stroke, so some slip is
  unavoidable; the weights accept a little skating in exchange for walking at all.
* lift is a shaping term (it rewards a means, stepping, not the goal). It is capped and small
  (0.3 max vs 3 per unit of progress), so it cannot outweigh walking.
* Reliability: seeds 0-4 all walk with the final fitness, but before the idle-leg penalty about
  1 run in 5 settled on standing still, so a failed run is still possible on other seeds. That
  would be a search failure, not a fitness exploit (walkers score 5-10, standing 0.2). Running
  several seeds and keeping the best is the practical remedy.
* Static stability is kept but only just: every evolved gait's minimum margin is 0.00-0.02.
  MARGIN_CAP makes extra margin worthless beyond 0.2 and progress pays, so the GA walks on the
  edge. A real robot would want a minimum margin constraint (e.g. 0.1).
* With progress weighted 3, the collision penalty (2 x overlap) is cheap relative to speed: the
  hill climber refining the hand gait trades feet 0.20 apart for 4.1 per cycle. The evolved
  gaits keep feet >= 0.30 apart, but a stronger collision weight may be needed if that changes.
* Speed: the model's general Rodrigues kinematics takes ~0.4 s per 100 gaits, so fitness.py uses
  an exact closed form (each leg lies in a vertical plane; asserted equal to spider_model).
  A population of 100 evaluates in ~0.2-0.3 s.
