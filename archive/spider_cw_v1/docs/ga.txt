GENETIC ALGORITHM (part1_ga/ga.py)
==================================

Purpose
-------
ga.py is a generic real-valued GA. It does not import the gait or fitness code; it is given an
evaluate_population(pop) function plus the gene bounds LOW/HIGH and a mask of circular genes.
That keeps it testable: `python -m part1_ga.ga` checks it on 72-D test functions (shifted sphere,
Rastrigin) with the same bounds as the gait before it is used on the spider.

The loop
--------
Generation 0 is drawn uniformly inside the bounds (no bias towards any region). Then, each
generation:
  1. evaluate   - score the whole population (higher is better; negative scores are allowed)
  2. record     - best / mean / worst score, sigma, the best chromosome and its fitness components
  3. terminate? - stop at the generation limit, after `stall_generations` without a new best,
                  or when `target_fitness` is reached
  4. elitism    - copy the top `elitism` (default 2) individuals unchanged
  5. selection  - choose parents for the remaining pop_size - elitism children
  6. crossover  - pair parents, cross each pair with probability crossover_rate (0.9), else copy
  7. mutation   - Gaussian noise on a few genes
  8. repair     - clip normal genes to their bounds, wrap circular genes (phases)
All operators are vectorised with numpy and use one np.random.default_rng(seed), so a run with
the same seed is exactly reproducible (asserted in the self-check).

Selection
---------
Tournament (default): each parent is the best of k random individuals (k=3). It only compares
scores, so it is unaffected by negative values or by the scale of the fitness, and k gives direct
control of selection pressure (k=2 gentle and diverse, k>=5 fast but risks premature convergence).
Roulette wheel (offered for comparison): probability proportional to fitness. Because scores can
be negative they are shifted by the population minimum, plus a small epsilon so the worst
individual is still selectable and so equal scores do not divide by zero (then selection is
uniform). Its weakness is scale dependence: one outlier can dominate early, and late in the run,
when scores are close, pressure almost disappears. The test table shows this: roulette converges
more slowly than tournament on every operator.

Crossover
---------
BLX-alpha blend (default): each child gene is drawn uniformly from [min - a*d, max + a*d] where
d = |parent1 - parent2| and a = 0.5. It suits real-valued genes: it creates new values between
and slightly beyond the parents (exploration), and the interval shrinks automatically as the
population converges (fine tuning). For circular phase genes the second parent is first moved to
its nearest equivalent (0.1 and 6.2 rad are 0.18 rad apart, not 6.1), otherwise blending two
nearly equal phases would produce the opposite phase.
Two-point: swaps the segment between two random cut points. It keeps blocks of neighbouring
genes together; in our encoding a leg's three joints (coxa, femur, tibia) are adjacent, so a
good leg configuration survives as a unit. Less mixing than uniform.
Uniform: each gene from either parent by coin flip. Maximum mixing and ignores gene order, but
like two-point it can only recombine existing values; new values must come from mutation.

Mutation
--------
Each gene mutates with probability mutation_rate (0.05, about 1-2 of the 27 gait genes per child) by
adding Gaussian noise with std = sigma * (HIGH - LOW). Scaling by the gene's range lets one sigma
work for genes with very different ranges (0.4 rad amplitudes, 2*pi phases). Gaussian noise gives
mostly small tweaks with occasional larger jumps.
Adaptive rule (adaptive_mutation=True):
    sigma = sigma0 * (1 - 0.9 * gen / generations) * 0.5 ** (stall / stall_generations)
floored at 5% of sigma0. Large steps early explore; later the population sits near a good gait
and large steps mostly break it, so sigma decays linearly to 10%. If the best has not improved
for a while we are likely on a peak needing finer moves, so sigma also halves per
stall_generations of stalling; the stall count resets whenever the best improves.

Elitism and termination
-----------------------
Elitism guarantees the best gait is never lost, so the recorded best score never decreases
(asserted). Two elites out of 100 is enough for that without letting them flood the gene pool.
The run stops at `generations` (default 300), after `stall_generations` without improvement,
or when a `target_fitness` is reached. stall_generations defaults to 300, i.e. off: on the gait,
walking often only emerges after 60-200 generations of slow improvement from a stable stance
(docs/fitness.txt), and the old default of 50 ended such runs while the best was still standing.

Repair of circular genes
------------------------
Phases (the 4 phase genes, gait.WRAP; genes 48..71 in the original 72-gene encoding) live on a circle: 0 and 2*pi are the same gait. Clipping would pile
individuals up at the two ends and stop the GA from moving "through" 0, so these genes are wrapped
modulo 2*pi instead (low + mod(x - low, range)). All other genes are clipped, because their bounds
are physical joint limits. The self-check places the test optimum at phase 0.05 rad, right next
to the wrap point, and confirms it is found.

Tuning guide
------------
pop_size / generations : more = better search but cost scales with pop_size x generations.
tournament_k           : raise for faster convergence, lower if the population collapses early.
crossover_rate         : 0.7-0.95 typical; lower keeps more parents intact.
blend_alpha            : 0 = only between parents, 0.5 = standard, higher = more exploration.
mutation_rate / sigma  : raise if the best stalls early with low diversity (mean close to best);
                         lower if the best is noisy and good gaits keep getting destroyed.
stall_generations      : larger to allow escapes from plateaus, smaller to stop early.

Every combination improves greatly. Tournament beats roulette throughout (stronger, scale-free
pressure). Uniform does best on these test functions because they are separable (each gene can
be optimised independently); on the gait, genes interact within a leg, which is where two-point
and blend are expected to help. Roulette + blend is the weakest pair: weak late pressure combined
with blend's variance-preserving sampling keeps the population spread out.

Results on the gait (python -m part1_ga.experiments: 5 seeds, 150 generations, pop 100 unless
varied, every other setting at the GAConfig default, 27-gene encoding; plots in
outputs/part1/experiments/)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

selection    tournament    6.04 +- 1.57   (default)
selection    roulette      1.46 +- 0.56
crossover    uniform       4.54 +- 2.52
crossover    two_point     2.38 +- 1.23
crossover    blend         6.04 +- 1.57   (default)

population   50            1.19 +- 1.90
population   100           6.04 +- 1.57   (default)
population   200           7.12 +- 2.22
(For scale: standing still scores 0.2, the hand-built tetrapod 3.9.)

* Tournament >> roulette (6.0 vs 1.5). The fitness spans about -12..+10 with large negative
  penalties, so roulette's shifted proportional weights give almost flat selection pressure
  once the population is walking.
* Blend crossover is best, and on the gait the test-function ranking changes: two-point, which
  cuts the chromosome into blocks, is the worst here. A leg's pose, its amplitudes and the
  shared phase genes sit in different blocks but only work together, so block swaps break
  working combinations; blend samples between and around two good parents and keeps them
  coherent. (With the earlier 28-gene encoding the order of uniform and two-point was reversed,
  with both well below blend; blend's lead is the consistent result.)
* Mutation rate 0.05 is a sweet spot: 0.01 explores too little, 0.2 disrupts good gaits. The
  adaptive sigma has a similar mean to a fixed sigma (6.0 vs 5.8) but half the spread between
  seeds (std 1.6 vs 3.0): decaying sigma lets every run settle instead of a lucky few.
* Population 50 is too small to find the coordinated walk reliably. 200 scores a little higher
  than 100 (7.1 vs 6.0, inside one std) for twice the evaluations, so 100 stays the default.
* With 5 seeds the stds are large; differences under about one std are not conclusive.
